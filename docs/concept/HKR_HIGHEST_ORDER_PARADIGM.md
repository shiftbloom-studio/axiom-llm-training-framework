# HKR — Highest-Order Paradigm

This is the highest-order paradigm of this project. Every other document, concept,
decision, and implementation is subordinate to it. Wherever anything drifts toward a
less fundamentally different reading, it is wrong and must be corrected back to this
document.

## Intent

This states the upmost, most hypothetical and radical version of the HKR thesis — the
one this project is deliberately trying to implement. It is pursued even knowing it is
not yet proven and might deliver no usable results; the most-fundamentally-different
thesis of HKR nonetheless has indications from recent research as well as a high
internal consistency.

## Paradigm 1 — The context field is open-dimensional

Around the fact-core there is a field of context. This is not a simple two-dimensional
field where one can easily say "this influence affects it in that way." Each contextual
influence factor that exists is its own dimension — for example: ontic compatibility
(compatibility with the currently known world), the social or cultural context, the
amount of supporting evidence. These contextual influence factors are not limited to a
generalized, predefined list; they are undefined in number and can vary with the kind of
claim.

So the field around the fact-core is not two-dimensional, and not three-dimensional: in
the end, every contextual influence is an additional dimension that can affect the
fact-core and, ultimately, what is observed. The thinking must rather be like how the
context of an AI such as an LLM also affects its output — not determined by predefined
factors, but by how complex and different a context can be.

## Paradigm 2 — The fact-core is the centering of the context field, and is not invariant

Within this complex context field, the fact-core centers within the field. The fact-core
means really the general reality. Given that these contexts are subjective and have — in
technical terms — different parameters that will be different for every observer and
their contextual influences, the fact-core centers within the median of those contextual
influences.

This ultimately means that the fact-core itself is not invariant. The fact-core — the
actual "objective" reality — drifts, or more simply alters, along the axis of time.

## The core of the paradigm

This, in the end, is really what the fundamentally, radically different paradigm of HKR
is about: not only perception, but basic reality itself transforming over time.
